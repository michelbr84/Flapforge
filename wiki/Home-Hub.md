# The Home Hub

The first screen after the boot is the home hub. It reads like the front page of an arcade
roguelite rather than a list of options: what you will fly next is on screen, so is what you
are about to unlock, and one gold button starts the run.

![The home hub with a fresh profile](images/hub.png)

*A fresh profile: Green Fields selected, level 1, the economy tree one level away.*

## Top to bottom

| Element | What it shows | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Player card** (top left) | Your selected bird as an avatar, "Player", a crown with your level, the XP track and the prestige badge once you have prestiged | The **Profile**: the same header, your lifetime statistics, the run history and the prestige panel |
| **Coin chip** (top centre) | Your coins, rolling up when a run just paid | The **Shop** |
| **Gear** (top right) | — | **Settings** |
| **Emblem and title** | The bird on the anvil, "Flapforge", "Arcade roguelite" | — |
| **World plaque** | "World 1 · Green Fields ›": the world the next run is played in, with a swatch of its palette | The **World Select** — see [[Worlds and Bosses]] |
| **Forge scene** | Your bird on the anvil, on a floating island in the selected world's palette, over that world's backdrop | — (it grows, see below) |
| **Next unlock** card | The nearest unlockable you can still earn, with its counter ("Economy 1 / 3") and a gold track | The screen where that kind of thing is earned or bought: Birds, World Select, Forge, Goals or the Shop |
| **START RUN** | The gold call to action, with "World • Difficulty" under it | The run, in the world and at the tier the plaque and the Birds screen name |
| **Last run line** | "Last run: 12 gates · +48 coins   Best: 31" — or an invitation to press START RUN | — |
| **Bottom navigation** | Shop · Birds · **Play** · Forge · Goals | Each item opens its screen; Play starts the same run as START RUN |

### The forge grows with you

The scene in the middle is a picture of your meta-progression. Every level bought in the upgrade
trees (see [[Shop and Upgrades]]) adds to it:

| Upgrade levels bought | The forge |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | The island, the anvil and your bird, cold |
| 1 | The hearth lights with a pulsing glow and a hammer leans on the anvil |
| 6 | A barrel |
| 14 | A banner in the world's accent colour |
| 22 | A second barrel and a brazier |
| 36 | Gold trim on the anvil, a taller flame and the occasional spark |

A prestiged profile carries the gold trim at any stage. The island, the sky and the letterbox
follow the selected world, so Storm Sky is a dark island under lightning and the Void is
almost black.

### Forge, Goals and the other names

- **Forge** on the navigation opens the whole upgrade workshop — the flight, economy and forge
  trees, on the screen titled *Upgrades*. It is not the forge tree alone, nor the Iron Forge
  world, nor Cinder the forge bird.
- **Goals** gathers the seven challenges, the 41 achievements, the milestones and the
  collections in four tabs — see [[Challenges and Goals]].
- **Birds** is where you pick the bird, its colours, the world row, the difficulty tier, the
  run mode and the loadout — see [[Birds and Abilities]] and [[Game Modes and Difficulty]].
- The **Profile** behind the player card is where the two-step prestige lives.

## Keyboard, mouse and touch

- Arrows and `Tab` move the focus: START RUN is focused on entry, `Down` reaches Play in the
  navigation, `Left`/`Right` walk the row and wrap at its ends, `Up` climbs to the next-unlock
  card, the plaque and the HUD band. `Enter` or `Space` activates; every control also reacts
  to hover, click and tap.
- `Esc` (or Back on Android) asks "Press again to quit"; a second press within three seconds
  closes the game. Opening any screen in between cancels the request. On the desktop,
  Settings › About also has a Quit row.
- Leave the hub alone for twenty seconds and a bot plays a demo run behind it, dimmed under the
  hub's chrome. Any key, click or pointer movement brings the hub back.

> **Tip:** the hub is live. Buying a bird in the Shop, selecting a world, prestiging or
> finishing a run is reflected the moment you return — no restart, no re-entry.

## Without a profile

When the game runs without a save (the headless launch, some tests), the hub still shows the
player card, the gear and START RUN, greys out Shop, Birds, Forge and Goals, and hides the coin
chip, the plaque and the next-unlock card. Everything else behaves the same.
